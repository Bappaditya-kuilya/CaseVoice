Runs a "council" deliberation — independent first-pass opinions from 5 distinct persona advisors, anonymized peer review/ranking, and a Chairman synthesis with a clear recommendation. Modeled on Karpathy's llm-council (github.com/karpathy/llm-council), adapted to one model simulating 5 differing perspectives instead of calling 5 LLM providers. Use whenever the user asks for "a council," "a panel," "multiple perspectives," wants something "peer reviewed," asks Claude to "not just agree with them," or is making a real decision (should I do X, is this a good idea, which option, review my plan/pitch/idea) where one agreeable answer risks missing objections. Trigger even without the word "council" — e.g. "poke holes in this," "steelman both sides and tell me what to do," "I don't want a yes-man answer." Skip this for simple factual questions or quick lookups — it's for decisions/plans substantial enough to be worth 5 distinct takes.
